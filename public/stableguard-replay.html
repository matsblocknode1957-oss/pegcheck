Depeg protection for stablecoin vaults

Freezes on a confirmed depeg. Reopens on its own.

StableGuard checks the stablecoins in your vault against three price sources. When they agree a coin has broken its peg, it blocks new deposits in a single transaction. Withdrawals stay open, so depositors can always take their money out. When the price holds again, it reopens deposits without anyone signing anything.

How it decides

It only moves when the evidence does

This is how the current workflow decides. It runs in simulation today; the dated record below shows what has run live.

Three sources, one median

Every check reads the Chainlink price feed, CoinGecko and DefiLlama, then takes the median. Each Chainlink node does this on its own, and the network settles on the median of their answers.

It only acts on agreement

With three sources, a single bad price is outvoted by the median. If only two sources answer and they differ by more than 1%, the signal is capped at WATCH and nothing is frozen.

Blocks deposits, never moves funds

In the tested setup, a freeze blocks new deposits while withdrawals stay open. A vault's admin can choose a full pause instead. StableGuard's role can only flip those switches. It has no path to the money.

It reopens on its own

Once the price is back at peg for 3 reports in a row, the hold is released and the vault reopens. The incident record closes 30 minutes later. Nobody has to sign anything.

Signal levels: WATCH from 0.2% off peg, ELEVATED from 0.5%, CRITICAL from 1%. Which level triggers protection is a configuration setting.

What's been shown

Dated, recorded and checkable

  1. 29 Oct 2026

    Rush alarm live on 20 Morpho markets

    Read-only alerts now run every 30 minutes on the 20 largest stablecoin lending markets on Morpho. Tested first on three real collapses (xUSD, sdeUSD and Resolv), where it warned 13 to 41 hours before the lock-ups, and on 36 healthy markets over 90 days to measure false alarms. Alerts go to a human; nothing is frozen automatically.

  2. 21 Oct 2026

    Multi-source workflow, run in simulation

    The upgraded Chainlink CRE workflow read the live Chainlink USDC feed, CoinGecko and DefiLlama, and they agreed within 4 bps. Test scenarios showed a confirmed depeg reaching CRITICAL, and a two-source disagreement held at WATCH. Not deployed live yet.

  3. 18 Oct 2026

    Automatic recovery, rehearsed on a fork

    On a copy of the live testnet contracts, healthy USDC reports were fed in. After the third in a row, the hold was released and the vault reopened with no manual step. After the 30-minute cooldown the incident closed as NORMAL. Nothing was sent to the real testnet.

Not built yet

What's still ahead

  • A human override for your multisig

    Designed so your own signers hold it: one signer can keep a vault locked longer, while unlocking early needs your full threshold and a reason recorded on-chain. Being built next.

  • Multi-chain protection

    The state machine already tracks vaults on several chains, but real cross-chain delivery over Chainlink CCIP hasn't been exercised yet.

  • Mainnet, timing and an audit

    StableGuard runs on testnet only. End-to-end timing hasn't been measured, and the contracts haven't had an independent audit.
